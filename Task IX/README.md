## Objective
Analyze sales, customers, products, and categories using SQL aggregate functions and business reports.

## Requirements Covered

2. Total sales reports
3. Top customers based on purchase amount
4. Best-selling products
5. Category-wise sales analysis
6. Customer sales summary
