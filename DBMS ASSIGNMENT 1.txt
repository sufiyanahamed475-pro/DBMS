Flipkart Website

1. Introduction

Flipkart is one of India's leading e-commerce websites. It allows users to shop for electronics, fashion, groceries, home appliances, and many other products online.

2. Functional Requirements

- User Registration and Login
- Product Search and Filter
- Add to Cart and Wishlist
- Secure Online Payment
- Order Tracking
- Customer Reviews and Ratings

3. Non-Functional Requirements

- Fast Response Time
- Secure Payment and User Data Protection
- User-Friendly Interface
- High System Availability
- Scalable Performance for Large Numbers of Users

4. Conclusion

Flipkart is a reliable and easy-to-use online shopping platform that offers a wide variety of products with secure payments and efficient delivery services.
